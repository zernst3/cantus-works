Privacy

Last updated November 2026. Cantus Works is a one-person practice run by Zachary Ernst in New York. "I" on this page means me. Questions go to hello@cantusworks.com.

What this page covers

This website and the messages you send me. Code you share for an inspection is covered by the agreement we sign before any work starts, which sets out how I handle it and when it is deleted.

What I collect

  • Visits · I use Umami to count visits without cookies: the pages viewed, the site that sent you, your browser, device type and country, and clicks on the sample download. It does not identify you or follow you across other sites. Umami stores this data in the United States, and I keep it for six months.
  • Hosting · GitHub Pages serves this site. GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address to keep its service running and secure.
  • Messages · The quote form is handled by Formspree, which passes what you write to my inbox. My email runs on Google Workspace, and calls are booked through Google Calendar's booking page. I keep what you send to answer you and, if we work together, as part of my business records.

Why I use it

Visit counts tell me which pages are useful (my legitimate interest in running the site). Messages are used to answer your request and to prepare an agreement if you ask for one.

Who else sees it

Only the services named above, acting for me. I don't sell your information, use it for advertising or share it with anyone else. These services may store data in the United States and rely on their own safeguards for international transfers.

How long I keep it

Visit counts for six months. Messages for as long as needed to answer you and for as long as business and tax records must be kept.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what I hold about you, or object to how I use it, by writing to hello@cantusworks.com. If you are in the EU or the UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Cookies

This site sets none.
